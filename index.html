Day In The Life

Cadet's Daily Routine

Follow the structured daily lifestyle of a student at Deep Public School. Time management and discipline form the foundation of our culture.

05:30 AM - 07:30 AM

Morning Drill Call

Cadets start the day with jogging, physical training (PT), yoga, marching drills, and self-defense exercises on the main field.

07:30 AM - 08:30 AM

Breakfast & School Assembly

Uniform inspection, national prayer sessions, and a healthy breakfast in the communal dining hall to energize for academic classes.

08:30 AM - 02:00 PM

Academic Study Hours

Core classroom education focusing on sciences, math, English fluency, and computer labs, featuring digital interactive screen sessions.

02:00 PM - 04:30 PM

Lunch & Mandatory Rest

Pure vegetarian lunch menu cooked in hygienic steam kitchens, followed by a quiet resting period in the hostels.

04:30 PM - 06:30 PM

Outdoor Sports & Obstacles

Athletics coaching, volleyball, football, cricket matches, and military obstacle courses to build coordination and teamwork.

06:30 PM - 09:30 PM

Supervised Study Prep

Dedicated library reading time, completing assignments, and exam tutoring supervised by residential subject teachers.

09:30 PM Onwards

Lights Out & Rest

Roll call checks in dormitories and restful sleep to ensure students recover and remain energized for the next day's routines.
